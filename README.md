# Do Not Impose Your Conscience on Others

A 28-day personal Bible study about respecting another person’s conscience, forgiving ordinary shortcomings, and recognizing when serious wrongdoing needs appropriate spiritual help.

## Study program

Each day includes Bible reading, a link to supporting JW.org material, reflection questions, a practical step, and a personal journal.

- **Week 1:** Train my own conscience.
- **Week 2:** Respect another’s conscience.
- **Week 3:** Discern with humility.
- **Week 4:** Respond with love.

The app includes a 10-minute timer, completion tracking, printable daily lessons, and journal export.

## Use the app

Open `index.html` in a browser, or publish it with GitHub Pages. No installation or build process is required. Bible and research links require an internet connection.

Journal entries and completion marks are stored in your browser on your device. They are not uploaded by the app and do not sync between devices. Export your journal before clearing browser data. If browser storage is blocked, copy or export your notes before leaving.

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. Upload `index.html` to the root of the `main` branch.
2. Open **Settings → Pages**.
3. Choose **Deploy from a branch**, select **main** and **/(root)**, then save.
4. Wait for GitHub’s Pages deployment to finish.

The expected address is:

[Open the conscience study]([https://ckcgoodnews-oss.github.io/conscience-study/index. html](https://ckcgoodnews-oss.github.io/conscience-study/))

This address becomes available after a successful Pages deployment.

## Sources and purpose

Bible links point to the New World Translation on Watchtower Online Library. Research links point to JW.org publications, including material on conscience, forgiveness, and responding to serious wrongdoing with love and mercy.

This version was rebuilt using the existing Personal-Relationship website’s app framework because the earlier downloadable study could not be recovered. The daily reflection prompts are original study questions.

This is an unofficial personal study aid, not produced or endorsed by Jehovah’s Witnesses or the Watch Tower Bible and Tract Society. It does not determine congregation cases or replace current publications and appropriate spiritual help.
