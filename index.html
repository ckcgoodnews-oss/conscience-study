A guide for discernment

Conscience choice: Where Scripture allows a personal decision, respect another person’s responsibility before Jehovah.

Shortcoming: Ordinary imperfections and minor personal offenses call for patience, forgiveness, and sometimes a kind conversation.

Serious wrongdoing: A clear serious violation of Scriptural standards needs appropriate spiritual help. Do not turn dislike, rumor, or a differing preference into an accusation. Congregation decisions belong to the appointed elders, not this app.

This guide is a study aid, not a case classifier. Read the linked current publications and consider the facts and context.
